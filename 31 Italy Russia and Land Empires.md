## Before

Episodes 25–30 followed maritime empires. Two other powers fit the maritime model awkwardly. Italy was a late maritime empire, and Russia was a land empire that expanded across a continent.

## The turn

Italy took Eritrea in the 1880s and Somalia in stages. It was defeated by Ethiopia at Adwa in 1896 (Episode 53). It invaded Ottoman Libya in 1911. Libyan resistance, led in Cyrenaica by Omar al-Mukhtar, continued into the 1930s. Italian forces confined much of Cyrenaica’s population in camps (1930–33), where tens of thousands died, according to estimates. Al-Mukhtar was hanged in 1931. In October 1935 Fascist Italy invaded Ethiopia, a League of Nations member. Its forces used mustard gas against soldiers and civilians. That is a documented fact of the war, and no technical detail is needed. After an attempt on Viceroy Rodolfo Graziani’s life in February 1937, Italian forces and Fascist militia massacred Addis Ababa residents (Yekatit 12); estimates of the dead run from the thousands to many more. Ethiopian resistance (the *arbegnoch*, “patriots”) continued until British and Ethiopian forces restored Haile Selassie in 1941.

Russian expansion began with Yermak’s campaign across the Urals in the 1580s. Cossacks and traders imposed fur tribute (*yasak*) on Siberian peoples. Epidemics and violence followed, as in the Americas. Russia reached the Pacific by the 1640s. In the 19th century it conquered the Caucasus, expelling much of the Circassian population in the 1860s, and Central Asia: Tashkent (1865), Samarkand (1868), Khiva (1873), Kokand (1876), and Geok Tepe (1881). Settler colonization of the Kazakh steppe provoked the 1916 revolt. Russia’s is a land empire that does not match the maritime model and that still dispossessed.

The Ottoman empire was not a European colony. It was another empire, and it lost territory to European powers. The Qing in 1700 was not a colony; it was an expanding empire. Both are named so viewers do not file them under “colonies.”

## Acts

### 1. Libya

The 1911 invasion, including the first aerial bombing in war (1911). Omar al-Mukhtar’s resistance, the Cyrenaica camps with estimated deaths, and Italian settlers. The 2008 Italy–Libya friendship treaty as a later acknowledgment.

### 2. Ethiopia, 1935–41

Invasion, gas stated as a fact, and Haile Selassie’s speech to the League of Nations (1936). Yekatit 12 with estimated tolls. The *arbegnoch*. The League’s failure. Ethiopia was occupied but never accepted colonial rule. It is not described as having been “colonized” in the same sense as others without that distinction.

### 3. Siberia

Yermak, *yasak*, and the fortress network. Indigenous peoples — Khanty, Evenki, Yakut (Sakha), Chukchi — as nations with their own resistance. The Chukchi resisted conquest successfully for decades.

### 4. Caucasus and Central Asia

The Circassian expulsion named with estimates and with its contested terminology. Conquests of the Central Asian khanates. The 1916 Central Asian revolt and its repression. Settler colonization of the steppe as a land-empire version of the settler model (Episode 5).

### 5. Not colonies

A clear graphic separating European overseas colonies from other empires: Ottoman, Qing, Mughal, and Russian as a land empire. The Ottoman empire was a sovereign empire that lost provinces to Europe. The Qing was an expanding power in 1700. The act prevents a common error.

## Comparative note

Russia’s land expansion resembles the United States’ continental expansion (Episode 46): settlers, forts, treaties, and dispossession across a continent. Neither needed an ocean.

## How we know

Italian military records and League of Nations documents. Ethiopian testimony and the Ethiopian government’s postwar documentation. Libyan oral history and the Libyan Studies Centre archives. Russian imperial archives. Scholarship by Angelo Del Boca, Ian Campbell (*The Addis Ababa Massacre*, 2017), Yuri Slezkine (*Arctic Mirrors*, 1994), and Alexander Morrison (*The Russian Conquest of Central Asia*, 2020).

## Do not claim

- That the Ottoman empire was a European colony, or that the Qing was one in 1700.
- That Ethiopia was a colony in the same sense as Eritrea; it was occupied in 1936–41 and resisted throughout.
- That Russian expansion was not colonial because it crossed land rather than sea.
- That death tolls in Libya, Ethiopia, or the Caucasus are exact.
- That the chemical weapons attacks require technical explanation; they are named as facts.
