# Seasons and paths

Packaging guide for *Colonies and Colonial Power* (*The Colonial Age*). It describes how the 72 episodes can be watched in full, sampled, taught, or followed by theme. It belongs to this series only.

Series bible: [`README.md`](README.md). Production: [`PRODUCTION_GUIDE.md`](PRODUCTION_GUIDE.md). Evidence: [`SOURCE_CATALOG.md`](SOURCE_CATALOG.md). Comparison: [`COMPARATIVE_MATRIX.md`](COMPARATIVE_MATRIX.md). Clearance: [`RIGHTS_AND_FACTCHECK.md`](RIGHTS_AND_FACTCHECK.md).

## The full run

Nine seasons of eight episodes, about 60 hours. The seasons move in this order: system, Iberian empires, Britain, other empires, the Atlantic machine, the Americas, Africa, Asia, resistance and after. Season 1 is required before any path, because it sets the vocabulary, the claim labels, and the method for numbers.

| Season | Episodes | Subject |
|---|---|---|
| 1 — The System | 1–8 | Word, world, line, charter, types, archive, numbers, refusals |
| 2 — Iberian Worlds | 9–16 | Atlantic islands to 1898 preview |
| 3 — Britain | 17–24 | Factories to ledger; the Raj once |
| 4 — Other Empires | 25–32 | Dutch, French, Belgian, German, Italian, Russian, Japanese, US |
| 5 — The Atlantic Machine | 33–40 | Capture to afterlives |
| 6 — The Americas | 41–48 | Collapse and survival to unfinished equality |
| 7 — Africa | 49–56 | States before the scramble to many Africas |
| 8 — Asia | 57–64 | Footholds to many clocks; the Raj refused |
| 9 — Resistance and After | 65–72 | Revolt to the unfinished ending |

## Paths that are the wrong series

### An India-only path

There is no India-only path through this series, and building one would be a mistake. This series films British rule in South Asia exactly once, as a system inside a world empire (Episode 21), and refuses a second time explicitly (Episode 62). Viewers who want the East India Company in South Asia, 1857, the national movement, Gandhi, Partition, and the republic should watch *Discovery of India*, a separate project that owns that history. Stringing together Episodes 21, 23, 62, and 63 would produce a fragmentary, misleading India series. Do not do it.

### A Britain-only path

Season 3 is about Britain, but a Britain-only path is not this series. The series’ thesis is that colonial power was a world system with many empires and that colonized people shaped its ending. Watching only British episodes reproduces the anglophone habit of treating one empire as “the” empire. Viewers who start with Season 3 should continue to at least Seasons 4, 5, and 9.

### Any empire-ranking path

No path is designed to compare empires morally. Paths compare institutions. See [`COMPARATIVE_MATRIX.md`](COMPARATIVE_MATRIX.md).

## Content guidance by episode

Episodes with sustained treatment of mass death, torture, sexual violence, or genocide carry a pre-title card and are flagged for educators: 10, 25, 29, 30, 31, 34, 36, 38, 46, 47, 63, 69. None contains reenacted violence. See [`PRODUCTION_GUIDE.md`](PRODUCTION_GUIDE.md).
