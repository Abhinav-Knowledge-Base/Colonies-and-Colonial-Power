### Series thesis

From Iberian oceans to industrial empires and mid-century independence, colonial power was a world system — of ships, companies, plantations, missions, and guns — met by resistance, adaptation, and eventually decolonization. This series moves by **empire**, by **region**, and by **system** (slavery), then closes on freedom struggles.
