# Ep draft — The Americas Under Colonial Rule

## Documentary treatment (draft)

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| **Logline** | Regional experience of conquest, slavery, mestizaje, and independence across two continents. |
| **Acts** | Contact & collapse → colonial societies → slave & indigenous labor → creole independence → unfinished equality |
| **Key topics** | Columbian Exchange; caste; maroon communities; Bolívar/US revolutions compared carefully |
| **Do not claim** | Colonialism as pure progress, or colonized peoples as only victims without agency |
