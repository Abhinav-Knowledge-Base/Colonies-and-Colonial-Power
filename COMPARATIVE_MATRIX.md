# Comparative matrix

Side-by-side criteria for *Colonies and Colonial Power* (*The Colonial Age*). The matrix compares institutions: type of colony, labor regime, legal status of the colonized, the decolonization clock, and what is still unfinished. It has **no moral ranking column**, and no cell should be read as one (honesty rule 4). It belongs to this series only.

Series bible: [`README.md`](README.md). Production: [`PRODUCTION_GUIDE.md`](PRODUCTION_GUIDE.md). Packaging: [`SEASONS_AND_PATHS.md`](SEASONS_AND_PATHS.md). Evidence: [`SOURCE_CATALOG.md`](SOURCE_CATALOG.md). Clearance: [`RIGHTS_AND_FACTCHECK.md`](RIGHTS_AND_FACTCHECK.md).

## How to read it

- **Type** uses the models from Episode 5: settler, extractive, plantation, trading-post, protectorate, or mixed. These are models, not laws (honesty rule 11). Many colonies moved between types.
- **Labor regime** names the dominant coerced or free labor systems. Most colonies had several.
- **Legal status** describes how the colonized stood in colonial law.
- **Clock** gives the main decolonization date or dates and how it came.
- **Unfinished** lists questions still open.
- Numbers are not included in the matrix. Tolls live in the episodes, with their labels.

## What the matrix cannot do

It cannot say which empire was worse. It cannot sum suffering across colonies. It cannot fix cells that the evidence leaves open. Where a colony changed type, the matrix shows its dominant form and the episodes show the change.
